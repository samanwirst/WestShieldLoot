# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-27

- Initial open-source release of WestShield's personal natural-container loot.
- Permanent account/container rolls with independent vanilla randomness.
- Shared Java/Bedrock consumption history through canonical account UUIDs.
- Take-only inventories and a durable delivery/recovery journal.
- Natural chests, double chests, barrels and chest minecarts remain destructible.
- Permanent destruction tombstones; replacement containers cannot refill rewards.
- Trial Vaults and player-created storage remain outside the mechanic.
- 64 local checks passed; production startup, status and database integrity verified.

The release JAR is byte-for-byte identical to the artifact installed on WestShield.
